## 1. Exact main theorem

`PomdpLogging/MainTheorem.lean` defines `PaperMainClaims H` and proves

```lean
theorem main_theorem (H : ℕ) (_hH : 3 ≤ H) : PaperMainClaims H
```

For the constructed candidate pair, its fields establish the dimensions, bounded
return, common logger, deterministic target, action coverage `6`, belief coverage
`3`, and inverse induced matrix 1-norm bounds `35/9` for G and `9` for K at `h < H`.
It proves target values `1/4` and `3/4` and the exact lower bound

\[
n\ge\frac{2\,\mathrm{kl}(1-\delta,\delta)}{p_H\log3},
\qquad p_H=\frac13(2/3)^{H-2},\qquad 0<\delta<1/2.
\]

The bound quantifies over every estimator accurate to `1/8` in both models.
`ValueEstimator` maps the entire finite data set to an arbitrary
`MeasureTheory.ProbabilityMeasure ℝ`; continuous output randomization is included.
The estimator is not restricted to the two target values or to a selected algorithm.
`ExactLowerBound.lean` proves the inequality using the exact full-data product KL.

The known candidate models and policies are fixed inputs. Use of their descriptions
and model-generated randomization is encompassed by the unrestricted output kernel.
Extra samples from the unknown environment are outside the paper's protocol.

## 3. Full-data minimax and sample complexity

`full_dataset_compression` and `reconstructs_full_dataset` prove both directions
of the three-symbol equivalence for every sample size. Reconstruction uses a kernel
common to both candidates. Thus no lower bound is obtained merely by voluntarily
discarding informative data.

`likelihood_is_majority` proves that the likelihood test is majority among the
non-erased observations, with fair tie-breaking. `majority_risk_formula` gives its
exact tail probability. `exact_full_data_minimax` proves a lower bound for every
real-valued randomized estimator and attainment on the full data.

The exact risk bounds are

\[
\tfrac12(1-p_H)^n\le e_n\le
\tfrac12[1-(1-\sqrt3/2)p_H]^n.
\]

`accuracy_iff_risk` identifies accurate estimation with the minimax risk condition.
`exact_sample_sandwich` proves the KL lower bound and the stated ceiling/logarithm
upper bound for the smallest accurate sample size. `uniform_sample_rate` proves
explicit universal constants, including integer rounding, for `H >= 3` and
`0 < delta <= 1/4`:

\[
\frac49(3/2)^H\log(1/\delta)
\le N^*(H,\delta)\le12(3/2)^H\log(1/\delta).
\]

## 4. Appendix coverage

**Appendix C.** Proved: same-belief/different-future histories, action-law TV `1/2`,
failure of physical-belief factorization, augmented diagonal belief Gram, its rare
eigenvalue and exponential coverage cost, and arbitrarily long common suffixes
with persistent belief/logger distances. All exhibited histories have positive
logging probability. Reachable augmented states are indexed by logger memory;
the map to `(physical state, memory)` is proved injective.

**Appendix D.** The actual first-reset/no-reset-terminal-bit statistic has exactly
`2L+2` symbols. A state-independent reconstruction kernel is constructed, and
`compact_preserves_grams` proves equality of both full and compressed matrices.
The kernel may depend on the fixed candidate, as the paper permits. The compressed
channel is defined as the actual pushforward; its displayed standalone row tables
are not separately exported closed-form theorems.

**Appendix E.** The deterministic terminal-emission model and target values zero
and one are formalized. The actual compressed laws are `(1-p,p,0)` and `(1-p,0,p)`.
The exact full-data formulas

\[
\operatorname{TV}(P_0^{\otimes n},P_1^{\otimes n})=1-(1-p)^n,
\qquad e_n=\tfrac12(1-p)^n
\]

are proved, including minimax estimation over arbitrary randomized real outputs.
The auxiliary sentence extending revealing to deterministic emissions is not a
separate formal theorem: the noisy construction's revealing infrastructure uses
strictly positive entries, while the deterministic variant can have zero entries.
This limitation does not affect any main-theorem claim.

Figures, empirical tables, novelty, references, and every explanatory sentence
are not part of the Lean certification. The earlier independent review covers
those aspects. Its observation-augmentation wording clarification remains a
manuscript issue, rather than a failure of the proved coverage or hardness result.
